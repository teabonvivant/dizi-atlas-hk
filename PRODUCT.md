# 中國竹笛知識庫

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Chinese dizi learners, teachers, and readers interested in musical culture. The existing public content uses Hong Kong Traditional Chinese.

## Product Purpose

A public knowledge website connecting practical learning, listening, repertoire, musicians and research. The user explicitly wants a professional, trustworthy site with cultural character.

## Capabilities and Constraints

100 illustrated journal articles, 50 musician profiles, 103 repertoire entries, 25 technique entries, 10 research reports, 200 research topics, a glossary and instrument-care guide. Full-text search, category filters, pagination, accessible navigation and mobile data expansion already work.

The 22 September 2026 redesign request changes layout and frontend UI. Preserve all article/data content, illustrations, URLs, source links, metadata and public access. The user delegated visual decisions and asks for a modern, stylish, distinctive result.

## Brand Commitments

Current homepage direction: the user rejected the September magazine-style opening and asks for stronger artistic, literary and classical Chinese character, specifically referencing 茶藝老師網. This is a homepage correction: photographic bamboo-window study, warm paper and literary type. Other content and routes remain unchanged.

Name: 中國竹笛知識庫 / Dizi Atlas. Literary Hong Kong Traditional Chinese, original editorial illustrations and learning diagrams. No fabricated credentials or public development notes.

## Evidence on Hand

Content is committed in content/, data/ and lib/. Existing illustrations are in public/images/editorial/ and public/images/journal/. The current public website is the product baseline.

## Product Principles

- Cultural character and first impression matter alongside usefulness.
- Preserve readable Chinese prose and clear access to knowledge.
- Treat reading, searching and learning as distinct but connected tasks.
- Make mobile and keyboard use complete.
