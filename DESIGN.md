# Design System: 中國竹笛知識庫 / Dizi Atlas

## Overview

### Current homepage: the bamboo-window study

The user explicitly rejected the previous homepage's gallery opening and pinned 茶藝老師網 as the visual reference. On the homepage only, the following overrides supersede the earlier homepage rules: a full-bleed photographic study, white Chinese serif heading, overlaid navigation with a small cinnabar wordmark seal, warm paper reading sections, an ink-green learning section and a quiet footer without the oversized English wordmark. The implementation is app/home-classical.css, and the governing surface contract is docs/classical-home-direction.md. Interior routes retain the system below.

The scoped chamber and home tokens below describe this implementation. Its photograph is an art-directed scene, not documentation of a historic object or a technical fingering diagram. Text remains live HTML; the search, ten topic links, four featured articles and six learning routes remain available.

**Creative North Star: "The Contemporary Cultural Reading Room"**

The site pairs the scale and composure of a museum gallery with the practical clarity of a reading room. Original illustrations and Hong Kong Chinese serif headings establish its cultural character; sans-serif prose, controls and indexes make a substantial collection easy to read and navigate.

Surfaces are quiet and mostly flat. Fine rules and editorial hierarchy separate material without turning every section into a card. The same hierarchy works on stone-white paper and in the reader-selected evening theme.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Hong Kong serif display with readable sans-serif prose and controls.
- Original image plates, generous headings and restrained terracotta emphasis.
- Flat editorial lists, fine rules and clear differences between lead and supporting material.
- Responsive indexes and a complete mobile reading flow.
- Light and dark themes share semantic colour roles and visible focus states.

This refresh records the shared system in `app/globals.css`, `tailwind.config.ts` and the shared components, with homepage overrides in `app/home-classical.css`. `docs/classical-home-direction.md` supersedes the old homepage composition in `.impeccable/surfaces/app-page-tsx.md`; product and preservation requirements remain in `PRODUCT.md`.

## Colors

Stone-white and olive-black carry the reading experience; terracotta marks intent. The frontmatter preserves the source HSL values rather than approximating them as hex.

### Primary

- **Olive-black ink — primary:** wordmark, headings, navigation and primary actions; primary-foreground supplies the reverse text.
- **Terracotta — accent:** source links, active underlines, link feedback, focus and limited emphasis. Accent-foreground supplies reverse text on the accent button.
- **Homepage chamber roles:** chamber-accent is aged-wood emphasis; chamber-green grounds the music-room section. Home-seal supplies the small cinnabar identity mark.

### Neutral

- **Stone-white paper — background:** page field, header and footer.
- **Raised paper — card:** fields and bounded supporting surfaces; separation stays subtle.
- **Reading ink — foreground / card-foreground:** long text.
- **Stone wash — secondary / muted:** secondary surfaces and hover feedback.
- **Secondary ink — muted-foreground:** introductions, descriptions and metadata.
- **Fine rule — border:** section, index, table and item boundaries.
- **Field line — input:** the stronger edge used by inputs.
- **Focus — ring:** the same accent family, dedicated to keyboard feedback.
- **Homepage paper and ink:** chamber-paper, chamber-ink, chamber-muted and chamber-rule are scoped to the homepage reading sections. Home-hero-ink and home-header-ink remain pale over the photograph in both themes.

### Evening theme

Shared `dark-*` entries replace their corresponding root CSS variables when the root has the `dark` class. The field becomes green-black, text becomes pale stone, and terracotta becomes warm clay. The `dark-chamber-*` entries instead replace scoped chamber variables on the homepage; its open mobile menu follows the same evening palette, while desktop dropdowns retain warm paper and dark ink. Use semantic variables in components so both themes remain coherent. Light is the initial default; the reader's choice is saved as `dizi-theme`.

**The Semantic Colour Rule.** New component styling uses the existing semantic roles; it does not hard-code a light-theme colour into a shared surface.

## Typography

**Display Font:** self-hosted Dizi Serif HK, with Noto Serif HK, Songti TC and Microsoft JhengHei fallbacks.
**Body Font:** self-hosted Dizi Sans HK, with Noto Sans HK, Microsoft JhengHei, PingFang TC and system fallbacks.

The pairing gives Chinese titles a literary presence while keeping extended reading, labels and data calm. The bundled serif face declares weights 500–900; the sans face declares 100–900, both with swap loading.

### Hierarchy

- **Display:** home-display is the current photographic opening; home-display-phone applies at 560px and below. The older display token records the superseded home opening and is not a current page scale. The journal masthead retains display-journal.
- **Homepage type:** home-section, home-intro, home-deck and the two home-story roles use positive tracking; home-summary is the supporting-story prose. The lead-story heading uses balanced wrapping at every width. Responsive overrides and section-specific sizes are recorded in the sidecar; inner-route scales below are unchanged.
- **Headline:** the shared page hero uses the headline role. Article titles use a separate observed scale (`clamp(34px, 4.6vw, 64px)`, weight 600, line-height 1.5, tracking -0.025em).
- **Section:** the shared section heading uses the section role. Article section headings are smaller (29px, line-height 1.6).
- **Title:** the title role describes knowledge-index headings. Journal titles are 23px / 1.6 on desktop; featured-person names are 32px / 1.6.
- **Body:** the body token describes article paragraphs. General prose uses 16px / 2; summaries commonly use 14–15px with line-height 1.9–2.
- **UI / Label:** controls use the UI role; metadata commonly uses the label size. Small English wordmarks and image captions may use explicit positive tracking.

Chinese line breaking is strict. Headings use balanced wrapping and descriptions use pretty wrapping where supported. Article title phrases wrap at Chinese punctuation without making the entire title unbreakable. General reading blocks cap at 72ch; article prose has its own layout measure.

**The Type Role Rule.** Reserve the serif for display, headings and selected editorial decks; keep controls, data and long article paragraphs in the sans family. Preserve negative tracking on the existing inner-route headings; homepage headings use their own positive tracking.

## Layout

The main container is centred, full width up to 1440px, with the frontmatter page-inline padding. Narrow variants cap at 944px and 1120px. Section rhythm uses the section-block token. At 599px and below, the side gutter is fixed at 22px.

The homepage has a full-bleed photographic hero, 720–900px high on desktop, 700–850px at 780px and below, and 690–810px at 560px and below. Its reading measure caps at 1280px with 64px minimum gutters, changing to 40px at 1100px and 22px at 560px. The image uses cover cropping: centre 56% on desktop, 58% centre at 780px, and 80% centre at 560px to retain the flute. The former panoramic plate composition is superseded.

Homepage stories use a lead beside three supporting rows; at 780px the lead spans the width and supporting items form three columns, then become image/text rows at 560px. Those phone summaries remain 13px and clamp to three lines. The six learning routes use two columns, then one at 560px; intro, search and sources also stack at that width.

Shared editorial and index grids outside the homepage adapt by content:

| Pattern | Wide | At 899px and below | At 599px and below |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Journal articles | 3 columns | 2 columns | 1 column |
| Featured musicians | 3 columns | 2 columns | 1 column |
| Full musician index | 4 columns | 3 columns | 2 columns |
| Topic index | 5 columns | 3 columns | 2 columns |
| Learning index | 3 columns | 2 columns | 1 column |

Article reading uses a 240px sticky contents rail beside prose up to 740px, separated by an 8% gap. At 1199px the rail becomes 200px with a 7% gap. At 899px it becomes a static, wrapping list above the prose; the layout caps at 850px. Article body text reduces to 17px / 2.05 at 599px. The cover changes from a 2:1 crop to 3:2.

Shared page heroes pair title/copy with an image at 1.35:1 columns, then stack at 599px. Desktop navigation begins at Tailwind's xl breakpoint (1280px), except the homepage's 1120px override. Other existing utility breakpoints remain 640px, 768px and 1024px; data tables become mobile disclosure records below 1024px.

Maintain 44px control targets, explicit input labels, visible result counts and useful empty states. Keep tables inside their overflow regions rather than causing page overflow.

## Elevation & Depth

Editorial surfaces are flat at rest. Paper tones, spacing, fine rules and image crops establish depth. Do not add general card shadows to journal, musician or knowledge indexes.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Navigation dropdown:** `0 14px 30px rgb(0 0 0 / .12)`, used for the temporary desktop menu.
- **Focused skip link:** the soft shadow `0 18px 46px rgb(28 35 30 / 0.10)` makes the keyboard-only overlay visible.
- **Line:** `none`; bordered data and teaching containers retain their flat treatment.

## Shapes

The dominant form is square: buttons, input fields, pagination, category controls and image plates have no rounded corners. Fine borders are usually 1px; selected category and tab underlines are 2px.

Existing supporting cards, selects, table wrappers and empty states use the small 2px radius derived from the 4px base radius. Metadata badges remain pill-shaped. These are established exceptions, not a reason to round editorial image plates or sections.

## Components

### Buttons and text links

Square, quiet controls with clear intent. The six shared variants are recorded in frontmatter; default controls are 44px high, large controls 48px, and icon controls 44px square. Primary and accent fills use 90% opacity on hover; secondary uses muted at 70%; outline shifts to muted with a primary border at 40%; ghost shifts to muted and primary ink. The link variant strengthens its underline.

Buttons have a 2px focus ring with a 2px background offset, an active scale of 0.98 and 50% disabled opacity. The standalone editorial text link has a 44px minimum height, a fine underline offset by 7px and an optional arrow that moves 2px up and right.

### Fields

The standard input is a square paper field with the stronger input border, 44px height, visible caret and a 2px focus ring. Text is 16px below 768px and 14px above it. The home search uses a transparent, minimum-48px field inside top and bottom rules; its phone text is 16px, and the icon submit retains its accessible name and 44px minimum width. Keep accessible names even where the visible search treatment is minimal.

### Navigation and filtering

The inner-route sticky header uses an opaque page field and a bottom rule. Its minimum height is 92px, then 82px at 899px and 78px at 599px. Desktop navigation uses an accent underline for hover/current state; mobile links use accent colour and an underline. The collapsed menu is scrollable and closes on route changes, outside clicks or Escape.

The homepage header is absolute over the photograph, with a vertical seal and pale navigation; its minimum height is 94px, then 83px at 560px. Opening the mobile menu gives it an opaque theme-aware paper ground; header icon targets stay at least 44px square. Escape returns focus to the mobile toggle. Its footer uses a dark music-room treatment and hides the oversized English wordmark only on this route.

Category links and data tabs use a fine active underline. Pagination uses square outlined cells with a solid primary selected cell. Metadata badges use a light tint and pill outline; they are labels, not action controls.

### Editorial items and containers

Journal stories use an image, serif title, metadata and excerpt with a closing rule. Knowledge items and musician profiles use rules and typographic hierarchy on the page field. Existing Card primitives retain a small radius, paper fill, 1px border and 20px header/content padding for bounded supporting material. Cards have no default shadow.

Original illustrations remain the editorial and teaching material; the homepage adds its photographic opening. Keep meaningful image alternatives, captions, sources and learning diagrams. Diagrams remain uncropped reading material, capped at 560px within article prose.

### Interaction and access

Global colour, background, border and shadow transitions last 180ms. Button feedback uses 200ms ease-out; shared editorial arrow motion uses 240ms and image hover uses 650ms, both with `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`. Image hover scales to 1.025. The former home plate's 1000ms crop reveal is superseded by one 1800ms photograph settling from scale 1.035 to 1; homepage arrows use 220ms ease-out.

The global keyboard outline is 2px in the ring colour with a 4px offset. Homepage content uses chamber-accent with a 6px offset, reversed to pale ink within the hero; its header retains the global offset with pale or open-menu ink. Inputs and shared buttons have their component ring treatments. Keep the skip link, semantic landmarks, `zh-Hant-HK`, hierarchical headings, labelled icon controls and current-page semantics. Anchor targets retain a 7rem scroll offset beneath the sticky header.

Reduced-motion preference disables animation and transitions, restores automatic scrolling and removes hover transforms from images and editorial arrows. Keep motion limited to the existing feedback and image reveal.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** retain original illustrations, readable Hong Kong Chinese and clear source paths.
- **Do** use semantic colour variables for both reading themes.
- **Do** distinguish lead stories, supporting stories and indexes through type, spacing and image scale.
- **Do** preserve visible focus, labelled controls, 44px targets and the mobile reading order.
- **Do** use the existing square controls and fine-rule editorial lists, retaining small-radius supporting components where they already apply.

### Don't:

- **Don't** reintroduce the superseded green-academy palette, Noto TC display stack or 1280px global container.
- **Don't** turn every section or index entry into a raised card.
- **Don't** add decorative crosshairs, gratuitous numbering or invented decorative labels.
- **Don't** apply a single desktop crop, grid or unbreakable title treatment at every width.
- **Don't** add motion that bypasses the existing reduced-motion treatment.
